The current live navigation label ("College") was used as the primary unit type since it reflects the present site; the PDF term "School" is an official alternate label for the same unit.

This content was not used for any recorded fact and is flagged for human review — it may be outdated or copied from an unrelated template.
- Institution type (e.g. "public university") was not found stated in explicit terms on the pages reviewed; left as not confirmed rather than assumed from the third-party `universities.md` seed list.
