# Addis Ababa University

## Overview

- **Official website:** [https://www.aau.edu.et/](https://www.aau.edu.et/)
- **Institution type:** Public — described on the official site as "Ethiopia's first autonomous public university"
- **Main location:** Addis Ababa, Ethiopia (14 campuses; region not further stated)
- **Data status:** Needs review

Addis Ababa University (AAU) was established in 1950 as the University College of Addis Ababa (UCAA) and is described as the oldest and largest higher-learning and research institution in Ethiopia. As of the official "AAU at a Glance" page, the university comprises **7 Colleges, 7 Institutes, and 1 School of Law**, together supported by **69 departments, 27 schools, 14 centers, and 2 teaching hospitals**, running 66 undergraduate and 350 graduate programs (115 PhD, 180 Master's, 32 Subspecialty and 23 Specialty certificate courses in Health Sciences).

## Data Notes

- AAU's main site (aau.edu.et) is a JavaScript application whose "Colleges" academic navigation and the "/A-to-Z_listing" programs page, and the "/AAU-leadership" leadership roster, all returned empty content with no underlying data request completing (confirmed via network-request inspection: no API calls fired). This prevented confirming the individual names of the 7 Colleges and 7 Institutes, and their departments, directly from the university's own navigation.
- The undergraduate admissions site (admission.aau.edu.et) provided a confirmed list of 66 undergraduate programme titles, but groups them in unlabelled numbered blocks (I–X) without stating the owning College/Institute/School for most blocks. Only "College of Business and Economics (CoBE)" (block X, partially) and the Bachelor of Laws / School of Law (block IX, cross-referenced with the "1 School of Law" figure on the at-a-glance page) could be attributed with reasonable confidence. All other confirmed programme titles are recorded under "University-wide Programmes" rather than assigned to a guessed college, per project policy on unconfirmed programme ownership.
- Graduate programmes (350 reported) were not itemised in this batch given the same structural/labelling limitation on the admissions site; this is left for a follow-up pass once college/department attribution can be confirmed.
- Recommended human action: contact AAU's ICT/web office about the non-functional Colleges/A-to-Z/Leadership pages, or locate an official PDF prospectus/catalogue that names each of the 7 Colleges, 7 Institutes, and their departments explicitly, then reconcile with the programme list already collected here.
