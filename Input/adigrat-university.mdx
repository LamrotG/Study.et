# Adigrat University

## Overview

- **Official website:** [https://www.adu.edu.et/](https://www.adu.edu.et/)
- **Institution type:** Public — official site states "Adigrat University (AdU) is one of the Ethiopian public universities established in 2011"
- **Main location:** Adigrat, Tigray, Ethiopia (approximately 900 km north of Addis Ababa, per the official site)
- **Data status:** Needs review

The university's own "About Us" page states it currently has **6 colleges, one school, and one institute, with 48 departments** in total.
