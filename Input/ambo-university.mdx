## Data Notes

- The eStudent portal (estudent.ambou.edu.et) offers a College/Institute/School selector that returned full department and programme data only for the default-selected "College of Agriculture and Veterinary Sciences." Re-querying it for any other unit (tested: Business and Economics, Natural and Computational Sciences) consistently returned an HTTP 401 error in the browser, both via direct UI selection and script-triggered selection — this appears to be a portal-side session/authorization limitation rather than a data-entry gap, so it was not used for the other colleges; their department/programme data was instead sourced from the main site's individual college pages.
- College of Medicine and Health Sciences' page is dated "to this date (April 2023)" — over three years old at the time of this check — so some figures (staff counts, student counts) may be outdated; the department and active-programme list itself was still treated as current absent contrary evidence.
- The Waliso Campus page (https://ambou.edu.et/walisocampus/) currently renders only Lorem Ipsum placeholder text; its three schools are named in the main navigation and the eStudent portal's college selector, but no departments or programmes could be confirmed for them.
- Institute of Technology (Hachalu Hundessa Campus) is also referred to as "hhc" in the site's URL slug; its overview page lists academic programmes directly without naming constituent departments.
- Institution type (e.g. "public university") was not found stated in explicit terms on the "About Ambo University" page reviewed; left as not confirmed.
- Recommended human action: retry the eStudent portal for the remaining colleges/institutes/schools (Business and Economics, Medicine and Health Sciences, Natural and Computational Sciences, Social Science and Humanities, Institute of Education and Behavioral Sciences, Institute of Technology, School of Law, and the three Waliso schools) once/if its authorization issue is resolved, to obtain the same level of detail confirmed for Agriculture and Veterinary Sciences.
