# Arba Minch University

## Overview

- **Official website:** [https://www.amu.edu.et/](https://www.amu.edu.et/)
- **Institution type:** Not confirmed as "public" in explicit terms; the official site describes AMU as "one of National Research Universities" of Ethiopia
- **Main location:** Arba Minch town, Gamo Zone, SNNPR, Ethiopia (440 km southwest of Addis Ababa)
- **Data status:** Needs review

The official "About AMU" page lists the university's academic units as: Arba Minch Institute of Water Technology, Arba Minch Institute of Technology, College of Agriculture, College of Business and Economics, College of Natural Sciences, College of Medicine and Health Sciences, College of Social Sciences and Humanities, School of Behavioral and Pedagogical Sciences, School of Law, and Sawla Campus.
