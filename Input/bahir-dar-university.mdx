# Bahir Dar University

## Overview

- **Official website:** [https://www.bdu.edu.et/](https://www.bdu.edu.et/)
- **Institution type:** Not confirmed as explicitly "public"; formed by Council of Ministers regulation no. 60/1999 GC merging Bahir Dar Polytechnic Institute (1963) and Bahir Dar Teachers' College (1972), inaugurated May 6, 2000
- **Main location:** Bahir Dar, Amhara Region, Ethiopia
- **Data status:** Needs review

The official "About Us" page states BDU "comprises five colleges, five institutes, two faculties, three schools, and three academies, bringing the total number of academic units to 18," enrolling more than 25,970 students in 452 academic programs (113 undergraduate, 187 Master's, 115 PhD). Its named units are: College of Science, College of Agriculture and Environmental Sciences, College of Medicine and Health Sciences, College of Business and Economics, College of Education and Behavioral Sciences, Bahir Dar Institute of Technology, Ethiopian Institute of Textile and Fashion Technology, Institute of Land Administration, Institute of Disaster Risk Management and Food Security Studies, Blue Nile Water Institute (BNWI), Faculty of Humanities, Faculty of Social Sciences, School of Law, School of Earth Science, School of Educational Science, School of Teacher Education, Sport Academy, and Maritime Academy. This list differs from the site's main navigation menu, which additionally names an Institute of Biotechnology, a School of Materials Science and Engineering, and a School of Veterinary Medicine, and does not separately list Blue Nile Water Institute, School of Educational Science, School of Teacher Education, Sport Academy or Maritime Academy — see Data Notes.

## Data Notes

- BDU's own "About Us" page and its main site navigation menu name different, only partly overlapping sets of academic units. The About page's list (18 units) additionally includes Blue Nile Water Institute, School of Educational Science, School of Teacher Education, Sport Academy, and Maritime Academy, none of which appeared in the main navigation menu; conversely the navigation menu names an Institute of Biotechnology, School of Materials Science and Engineering, and School of Veterinary Medicine not listed on the About page. Both sets are recorded as officially named somewhere on the site; the five units unique to the About page were not individually researched in this pass due to time constraints.
- For most colleges/institutes/schools, official pages list programmes directly (by title) without always grouping them under named academic departments; where a source did not use the word "department," this file does not invent one.
- Institution type was not found stated as explicitly "public"; BDU was formed by a Council of Ministers regulation, consistent with (but not proof of) public status, so `institutionType` is left unset pending an explicit statement.
- Recommended human action: research Blue Nile Water Institute, School of Educational Science, School of Teacher Education, Sport Academy, and Maritime Academy in a follow-up pass; confirm the undergraduate Law degree title and the exact department structure of College of Business and Economics and School of Veterinary Medicine.
