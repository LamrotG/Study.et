# Mekelle University

## Overview

- **Official website:** [https://www.mu.edu.et/](https://www.mu.edu.et/)
- **Institution type:** Not confirmed
- **Main location:** Mekelle, Tigray Region, Ethiopia
- **Data status:** Needs review

The university's own site states it has **122 undergraduate programmes across 7 Colleges and 9 Institutes**. Only 4 of the 9 institutes were individually confirmed in this pass (Ethiopia Institute of Technology-Mekelle, Mekelle Institute of Technology, Institute of Pedagogical Sciences, Institute of Paleoenvironment and Heritage Conservation); the remaining 5 institutes were not identified.
