# Mizan-Tepi University

## Overview

- **Official website:** [https://www.mtu.edu.et/](https://www.mtu.edu.et/)
- **Institution type:** Not confirmed
- **Main location:** Mizan-Aman, South West Ethiopia Peoples' Region (SWERS), Ethiopia. The university also operates a separate Tepi Campus.
- **Data status:** Needs review

## Academic Units

### College of Business and Economics (CoBE)

**Official unit type:** College (established 2007 as Faculty of Business and Economics)
**Source:** [College of Business and Economics](https://mtu.edu.et/business-and-economics/)

#### Departments

- Department of Accounting & Finance
- Department of Cooperatives
- Department of Economics
- Department of Management
- Department of Marketing Management (opened 2021)

#### Programmes

- Undergraduate degree programmes confirmed in each of the 5 departments above.
- MSc in Accounting and Finance — Master's
- MSc in Development Economics — Master's
- Master of Business Administration (MBA), including specializations in finance, international business management, project management, and supply chain management, plus a general MBA — Master's

### College of Social Sciences and Humanities (CSSH)

**Official unit type:** College
**Source:** [College of Social Sciences and Humanities](https://mtu.edu.et/social-science-and-humanities/)

#### Departments

- 14 departments confirmed, including: Ethiopian Language and Literature-Amharic; English Language and Literature; Civics and Ethical Studies; Geography and Environmental Studies; History and Heritage Management; Psychology; Pedagogy; Sociology; Social Works; Governance and Development Studies; Journalism and Communication; and 3 recently opened local-language departments (Bench Noon; Sheko; Sheki Noono)

#### Programmes

- BA in each of the department fields listed above — Bachelor's
- MA in Geography and Environmental Studies, with specializations in Land Resource Management and Local and Regional Development Planning — Master's
- MA in Teaching English as a Foreign Language (TEFL), Literature, and Linguistics (under English Language and Literature) — Master's
- MA in Special Needs and Inclusive Education — Master's
- MA in Counselling (under Psychology) — Master's
- Additional Master's programmes exist under Educational Planning, but the list was not read to its end.
