## Data Notes

- **Security note:** the College of Medicine and Health Sciences subdomain (`cmhs.uog.edu.et`) was found to contain a large number of injected spam hyperlinks redirecting through compromised third-party (mostly UK primary school) websites to an apparent online-gambling domain (`istanaslot.org`). This strongly suggests the subdomain has been compromised with an SEO spam injection. No linked content was followed or used as a source; only the page's own visible text was read. Recommended human action: report this to the university's ICT/security team.
- Institute of Biotechnology and Institute of Amhara Studies were named in navigation but not individually researched due to time constraints.
- Most colleges list departments/schools without individually itemised programme titles.
- Recommended human action: itemise specific programme titles per department across all colleges; research Institute of Biotechnology and Institute of Amhara Studies; confirm institution type.
