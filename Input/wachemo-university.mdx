# Wachemo University

## Overview

- **Official website:** [https://www.wcu.edu.et/](https://www.wcu.edu.et/)
- **Institution type:** Not confirmed explicitly as "public," but the official site describes it as "one of Ethiopia's third-generation universities," categorized as a "Comprehensive University," and its College of Engineering and Technology was "established... under Regulation No. 222/2011 by the Council of Ministers of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia" — consistent with public status but not an explicit statement
- **Main location:** Hosaena (also spelled Hossana/Hosanna on different pages), Ethiopia. Some department contact addresses list "SNNPR" (the pre-2023 regional designation); the area has since been reorganized into Central Ethiopia Region.
- **Data status:** Needs review
