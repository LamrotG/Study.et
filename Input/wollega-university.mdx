# Wollega University

## Overview

- **Official website:** [https://www.wollegauniversity.edu.et/](https://www.wollegauniversity.edu.et/) (self-styled "Wallaga University" on-site)
- **Institution type:** Not confirmed
- **Main location:** Nekemte, Oromia Region, Ethiopia. The university also operates a Gimbi Campus and a Shambu Campus, each hosting their own Faculties.
- **Data status:** Needs review

The university's academic structure spans three campuses: **Main Campus (Nekemte)** with Colleges, Institutes, and Schools; **Gimbi Campus** with the Faculty of Social Science and Humanity, Faculty of Resource Management and Economics, Faculty of Natural and Environmental Science, and Faculty of Business and Development; and **Shambu Campus** with the Faculty of Agriculture, Faculty of Technology, and (also) a Faculty of Resource Management and Economics. Given the scale of this university (17 named academic units across 3 campuses), only the four main-campus Colleges and one Institute were researched to department level in this pass.
