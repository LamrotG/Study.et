## Executive Summary

Study.et is a student-first platform dedicated to helping people discover higher education opportunities in Ethiopia through trustworthy, well-organized information gathered from official university sources.

Study.et does not replace official university websites. Instead, it organizes verified information into a consistent experience designed around the questions students actually ask.

The platform begins as a university discovery experience and is intentionally architected to evolve into Ethiopia's most comprehensive higher education knowledge platform, including academic programs, departments, career exploration, AI-assisted guidance, admissions, scholarships, and contributor-driven content.

## Mission

To make discovering higher education opportunities in Ethiopia simple, trustworthy, and accessible by organizing verified information from official sources into one modern, searchable platform.

## Vision

To become Ethiopia's most trusted higher education platform, empowering students, parents, educators, and international learners to confidently explore universities, academic programs, and future career paths.

## North Star

Every feature, design decision, engineering choice, and content contribution should make it easier for students to discover, understand, and compare higher education opportunities in Ethiopia.

If a proposed feature does not directly help users achieve that goal—or clearly support that future vision—it should not be added.

## Why Study.et Exists

Students usually begin with dreams, not university names. Information is fragmented across dozens of institutional websites with different structures and terminology. Study.et bridges that gap by organizing verified information into one calm, student-first experience.

## Core Values

- Student First
- Trust Through Accuracy
- Simplicity
- Longevity
- Openness

## Product Philosophy

Study.et is a knowledge platform—not a university ranking site or marketing platform. It organizes information while respecting official university sources.

## Research Philosophy

Official university sources are the primary source of truth. Research and application code remain independent.

## Design Philosophy

Study.et should feel like a modern academic library rather than a SaaS product. Content takes priority over decoration.

## Engineering Philosophy

Build software that future contributors will understand without needing its original author.

## Governance

- Product Owner
- Lead Software Architect
- Research Assistant
- Contributors

## Documentation Philosophy

Documentation is part of the product and the handbook is the project's primary source of truth.

## Development Workflow

1. Read the handbook.
2. Review roadmap and ADRs.
3. Implement only the current milestone.
4. Document important decisions.

## Success Criteria

Users should quickly answer:

- What can I study?
- Where can I study it?
- Which universities offer it?

## Long-Term Vision

Study.et is intentionally designed to grow into Ethiopia's comprehensive higher education knowledge platform with programs, departments, AI, scholarships, admissions, contributor workflows, and an admin CMS.

## Relationship to the Handbook

This charter explains why the project exists. The remaining handbook documents define product strategy, engineering rules, architecture, content standards, search, SEO, roadmap, contributors, and ADRs.
