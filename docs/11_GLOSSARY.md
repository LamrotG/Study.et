# Purpose

This glossary defines the official terminology used throughout Study.et.

Its purpose is to ensure that contributors, designers, engineers, researchers, and AI assistants all use the same language when describing higher education institutions in Ethiopia.

Whenever a term in this glossary conflicts with informal usage, the glossary takes precedence.

---

# General Principles

Study.et describes institutions exactly as they officially describe themselves.

If an institution uses the word **School**, Study.et should not rename it to **College**.

Likewise, if an institution uses **Faculty**, that terminology should be preserved.

The glossary provides general definitions for contributors—not replacements for official institutional names.

---

# University

A legally recognized higher education institution that awards academic qualifications.

Examples

- Addis Ababa University
- Adama Science and Technology University
- Jimma University

---

# College

A major academic division within a university.

A college usually contains multiple departments and academic programs.

Examples

- College of Natural Sciences
- College of Engineering

Some universities may not use this structure.

---

# School

An academic division within a university.

Some universities use **School** instead of **College**.

Study.et preserves the official naming used by each institution.

Examples

- School of Law
- School of Medicine

---

# Faculty

Some universities organize academic units as Faculties rather than Colleges or Schools.

Study.et treats Faculty as an official academic unit.

Never rename Faculties.

---

# Institute

A specialized academic or research unit.

Examples

- Institute of Technology
- Institute of Peace and Security Studies

Institutes may contain departments or research centers.

---

# Department

The primary academic unit responsible for teaching a specific discipline.

Examples

- Department of Computer Science
- Department of Civil Engineering
- Department of Biology

Departments belong to Colleges, Schools, Faculties, or Institutes depending on the university.

---

# Programme

A specific course of study leading to an academic qualification.

Examples

- Bachelor of Software Engineering
- Doctor of Medicine
- Master of Architecture

Programs belong to departments or schools.

---

# Academic Unit

A generic term referring to:

- College
- School
- Faculty
- Institute

Use this term only when the exact institutional structure is unknown or when writing generic documentation.

---

# Undergraduate Programme

A program pursued after secondary education.

Examples

- Bachelor's Degree
- Doctor of Medicine (where applicable)

---

# Postgraduate Programme

An advanced academic qualification.

Examples

- Master's Degree
- PhD

---

# Institution Type

Describes the ownership or governance of an institution.

Current values

- Public
- Private

Future values may include additional categories if required.

---

# Public University

An institution funded or administered by the Government of Ethiopia.

---

# Private Institution

A non-government higher education institution officially recognized to operate.

---

# Campus

A physical location where a university delivers education.

Universities may have multiple campuses.

Future versions of Study.et may include campus-specific pages.

---

# Main Campus

The primary administrative location of an institution.

---

# Region

The administrative region in which the institution's main campus is located.

Examples

- Oromia
- Amhara
- Addis Ababa
- Sidama

---

# City

The municipality where the institution is located.

Examples

- Adama
- Addis Ababa
- Jimma
- Hawassa

---

# Official Website

The primary website officially operated by the institution.

Only official websites should be treated as authoritative sources.

---

# Official Source

Any information published directly by the institution or another trusted official authority.

Examples

- University website
- Official academic catalog
- Official admissions office

Unofficial blogs and third-party summaries are not considered official sources.

---

# Alternate Name

A commonly used variation of an institution's official name.

Examples

- ASTU
- AAU

Alternate names improve search but do not replace the official name.

---

# Slug

A URL-friendly identifier used within Study.et.

Example

```
addis-ababa-university
```

Slugs must be:

- Unique
- Lowercase
- Hyphen-separated
- Permanent

---

# Tag

A descriptive keyword used to improve search and filtering.

Examples

- Engineering
- Medicine
- Public
- Oromia

Tags are metadata and are not displayed as official institutional information.

---

# MDX Content

The published content format used by Study.et.

Each university owns a single MDX document containing structured metadata and content.

---

# Research Output

The raw information collected before publication.

Research output is stored separately from application content.

It is never served directly to users.

---

# Verified Content

Content that has been reviewed and confirmed using official sources.

Only verified content may appear on the public website.

---

# Search Index

A generated dataset used to power fast search.

The search index is built from published content—not research files.

---

# AI Knowledge Base

The verified collection of Study.et content used to answer AI queries.

The AI assistant should never use unpublished research.

---

# Contributor

Any person or AI system that improves Study.et.

Contributors may work on:

- Engineering
- Documentation
- Design
- Research
- Content

All contributors follow the same handbook.

---

# Architecture Decision Record (ADR)

A document describing an important architectural decision.

ADRs explain:

- The problem
- The options
- The chosen solution
- The consequences

---

# Decision (DEC)

A high-level project decision recorded in `10_DECISIONS.md`.

The DEC index summarizes decisions while ADRs explain them in detail.

---

# Definition of Done

The minimum quality standard required before work is considered complete.

The official Definition of Done is maintained in `02_RULES.md`.

---

# Future Terms

As Study.et grows, this glossary will expand to include:

- Career
- Admission Pathway

- Research Centre

- Student Organization

- Campus Map

---

# Revision Policy

This glossary is a living document.

New terms should be added whenever they become part of the platform.

Existing definitions should remain stable unless there is a compelling reason to change them.

Consistency across the handbook is more important than individual preference.
