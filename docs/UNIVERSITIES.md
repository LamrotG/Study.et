### Sources
Compiled from the Ministry of Education's public-university listings, Wikipedia's *List of universities and colleges in Ethiopia*, and university directory sites (AddisBiz, Ethiopia Temari). Because Ethiopian university domains change periodically, treat this as a starting point and verify against the Ministry of Education (moe.gov.et) for anything official (admissions, transcripts, accreditation).
